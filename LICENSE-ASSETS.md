## How the assets are produced

Nothing in this game was downloaded or traced: every sprite, sound effect and
glyph is generated by the project's own tooling in `Tools/artgen/`
(Python + Pillow). Re-running `python Tools/artgen/main.py` reproduces the
entire asset set byte-for-byte, which is also why the license can be this
permissive.
